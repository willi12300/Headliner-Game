# Headliner Simulation Principles

1. **The world continues without the player.** Bands, members, labels, agents, festivals and sponsors pursue their own goals every week.
2. **Important outcomes have causes.** Randomness selects between plausible outcomes; context determines probability.
3. **Truth and player knowledge are separate.** Scouting reveals estimates, ranges and partial traits rather than raw hidden values.
4. **Every save is deterministic from its seed.** The seed, RNG state, schema version and simulation version are stored with the save.
5. **Entities remember.** Relationships contain trust, respect, familiarity and memories that can decay or become permanent.
6. **Stories emerge over time.** Major outcomes develop through story arcs such as conflict, illness, breakouts, disputes and financial crises.
7. **The economy must remain bounded.** Fees, budgets, followers and popularity are tracked through long-term balance reports.
8. **History is permanent.** Important performances, contracts, departures, closures and discoveries remain available in career archives.
9. **The simulation is independent of the interface.** UI screens display state and submit decisions; they do not invent outcomes.
10. **Customisation has mechanical consequences.** Festival identity, values, branding and policies influence fans, artists, sponsors and councils.
